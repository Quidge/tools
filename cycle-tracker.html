TrackerCycle Calendar

Menstruation
The uterine lining sheds, resulting in a period. Typically lasts 3–7 days. Energy and hormone levels are at their lowest.
Follicular
Begins after menstruation. FSH stimulates follicle growth in the ovaries. Estrogen rises, energy increases, and the uterine lining rebuilds.
Ovulation
A surge in LH triggers the release of a mature egg, usually around day 14 in a 28-day cycle. This is the most fertile window, lasting roughly 1–3 days.
Luteal
After ovulation, the follicle produces progesterone to maintain the uterine lining. If no implantation occurs, hormones drop and the cycle restarts. PMS symptoms may appear.
