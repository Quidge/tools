Dutch Blitz Scorekeeper

Track scores across rounds for 2–8 players. Each round, enter cards played to the Dutch piles and cards left in the Blitz pile; we’ll do the math (+1 per Dutch card, −2 per Blitz card) and keep a running total.

Game settings

First player to reach this total wins. Default 75.

Players

2–8 players. Each player picks a deck (symbol + color) matching their physical Dutch Blitz cards. The Classic Green box has the first four; the Blue Expansion has the last four.
