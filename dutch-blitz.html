Dutch Blitz Scorekeeper

Track scores across rounds for 2–8 players. Each round, enter each player’s net round score (+1 per card played to the Dutch piles, −2 per card left in their Blitz pile). Running totals accumulate down the scoreboard with the most recent round on top.

Game settings

First player to reach this total wins. Default 75.

Players

2–8 players. Each player picks a deck (symbol + color) matching their physical Dutch Blitz cards. The Classic Green box has the first four; the Blue Expansion has the last four.
